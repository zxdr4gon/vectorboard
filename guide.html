Infinite canvas · Notebook · PDF markup

Think on a plane with no edges.

Vector Plane is a whiteboard and notebook that runs in your browser and installs like an app. Sketch, diagram, annotate PDFs and zoom forever without losing sharpness. No account, works offline.

Start in ten seconds

The pen is already selected. Everything else is one tap away in the tool rail on the left (or along the bottom on a phone).

  1. Draw. Drag with a finger, stylus or mouse. Stylus pressure makes lines thicker.
  2. Hold still at the end of a stroke and it snaps into a clean line, arrow, circle, rectangle or triangle. Keep dragging to adjust a snapped line.
  3. Pan and zoom. Pinch with two fingers or scroll the mouse wheel. Hold Space and drag to pan.
  4. Open the menu (top left) to save, export, change paper, add pages or switch to dark mode.

Everything it can do

Keyboard shortcuts are shown next to each tool. On touch screens every action also has a button.

Pressure-sensitive pen P

Smooth, curved ink with true variable width from your stylus. Adjustable stroke smoothing steadies shaky lines.

Highlighter M

Translucent ink that blends over text and PDFs. It never auto-snaps, so underlines stay exactly as drawn. Remembers its own colour.

Shape recognition

Pause at the end of a stroke and a wobbly drawing becomes a perfect line, arrow, ellipse, rectangle or triangle.

Two erasers E X

The stroke eraser removes whole strokes. The precision eraser cuts straight through a path and removes only what you touch. Imported pages are never erased by accident.

Shapes

Rectangle R, ellipse O, triangle ⇧T, diamond D, line ⇧L and arrow A, with tinted or solid fills and dashed outlines. Hold Shift for squares and 45° angles.

Text and text boxes T N

Click anywhere to type a label, or drop a rounded text box for diagrams. Double-click any text to edit it. Paste plain text straight onto the board.

Sticky notes S

Colour-coded notes for brainstorming and planning. They grow to fit what you write.

Connectors C

Drag from one item to another to link them. Lines stay attached as you move things, so mind maps and flowcharts stay tidy.

Select, move, resize V

Drag a box to select, drag corners to resize, Alt-drag to duplicate. Copy, paste, recolour, reorder or lock anything from the floating selection bar.

PDF and image import

Drag files onto the board, paste a screenshot, or use the import button. On a paper page each PDF page becomes its own locked sheet you can write on.

Pages

Mix infinite canvases with portrait and landscape paper pages in one board. Reorder by dragging thumbnails, duplicate, rename, export ranges.

Truly infinite

Zoom from 5% to 4000%. Everything is stored as vectors, so lines stay razor sharp at any zoom level.

8 paper templates

Blueprint grid, dot grid, graph, lined, Cornell notes, isometric, music staves and blank, set per page.

Laser pointer L

A fading red trail for presenting or screen sharing. It never touches your drawing.

Palm rejection

Use a stylus once and fingers switch to panning and zooming, so your hand can rest on the screen.

Light, dark or system

Dark mode flips black ink to white automatically so it’s always readable. Exports stay on white.

Undo and redo

Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+⇧+Z cover drawing, moving, styling, imports and page changes.

Never lose your work

Your board is saved in this browser as you draw, with room for large PDFs. Files make it portable.

Auto-save

Every change is stored on your device. Close the tab and come back later and it’s all there. Drawings from older versions are brought over automatically.

Save and open files

Save the whole board, pages and imported PDFs included, as one .json file. Ctrl+S keeps saving to the same file.

Export PNG, SVG or PDF

Export a page, a selection, a page range or every page. Pick 1×–3× resolution, transparent backgrounds, with or without the paper template. Multi-page exports arrive as one PDF or a ZIP.

Copy as image

Copy a page or selection straight to the clipboard and paste it into chat, docs or slides.

Install it like an app

Once installed, Vector Plane opens full screen from your home screen and works with no connection at all.

iPhone and iPad

Open the app in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Android

In Chrome, open the ⋮ menu and choose Install app. You can also use Install app in Vector Plane’s own menu.

Desktop

In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon at the right of the address bar. Installed, it can open board files directly.

Offline

The app, PDF import and exports are stored on your device after the first visit, so they keep working on a plane.
