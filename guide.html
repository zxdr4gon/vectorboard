Get started in 10 seconds

The pen is already selected. Just start drawing.

  1. Draw. Drag with your finger, stylus or mouse.
  2. Hold still at the end of a stroke for a moment and it snaps into a clean line, circle, rectangle, triangle or arrow.
  3. Pan and zoom. Pinch with two fingers, or scroll your mouse wheel. Hold Space and drag to pan.
  4. Open the top-right menu (the little arrow) to save, export, change paper and switch to dark mode.

Everything it can do

Tap any tool in the toolbar. Keyboard shortcuts are in brackets.

✏️Pressure-sensitive pen P

Smooth, curved strokes. With a stylus, harder presses draw thicker lines. Pick any colour and size.

🖍️Highlighter M

Translucent strokes that blend over text and PDFs. They never auto-snap, so underlines stay as you drew them.

✨Auto-snap shapes

Pause at the end of a stroke and your wobbly drawing becomes a perfect line, ellipse, rectangle, triangle or arrow.

🧽Two erasers E X

The stroke eraser removes whole strokes. The precision eraser cuts straight through a path and removes only the part you touch.

◻️Shapes

Rectangle R, ellipse O, triangle T and arrow A. Drag to size them.

💬Text nodes N

Drop a labelled box anywhere. Double-click (or double-tap on touch) to rename it.

🔗Connectors C

Drag from one shape to another to link them. The line stays attached when you move things, so it's great for mind maps and flowcharts.

📄PDF and image import

Drag a file onto the page or tap the image button. PDF pages become annotatable sheets. Drag corners to resize.

🔍Truly infinite canvas

Zoom from 5% to 4000%. It's vector-based, so lines stay razor sharp at any zoom level.

📐8 paper templates

Blueprint grid, dot grid, graph paper, lined paper, Cornell notes, isometric grid, music staves and blank.

🌙Dark mode

One tap switches between light paper and dark. Black ink flips to white automatically so it's always readable.

↩️Undo and redo

Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Shift+Z, or use the toolbar buttons. Covers drawing, moving, deleting and imports.

Never lose your work

Your drawing is safe, and portable too.

💾Auto-save

Everything is saved in your browser as you draw. Close the tab and come back later, and it's still there.

📁Save and open files

Save your whole canvas as a small .json file, including shapes, connectors and imported pages. Move it to another device or back it up.

🖼️Export PNG or SVG

Download a high-resolution PNG, or a scalable SVG for slides, print and design tools.

Built for touch

On phones the toolbar sits at the bottom within thumb reach, buttons are larger, and pinch-to-zoom works with two fingers. The menu collapses with the arrow button when you want the full screen to draw on.
